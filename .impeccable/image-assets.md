# Illustration assets

`src/assets/illustrations/workspace-folder.webp` was generated with the built-in
Imagegen tool and encoded to WebP using cwebp. The original output is retained at
`/Users/chamrong/.codex/generated_images/01a0f7f7-f078-77b0-9181-97498d96ea01/exec-a466d645-2f2e-4679-9288-dd48ed048f2d.png`.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: illustration for the right half of Anumat ERP workspace onboarding, 1024 square composition. Create an original tactile editorial illustration: a large mint-green paper folder holding two cobalt blue approval documents and a smaller lilac identity card, with a small green circular checkmark floating at the upper right. Slight hand-drawn irregular geometry, flat paper-collage volumes, subtle risograph stippling and grain, gentle dimensional folds. Colors cobalt #285ff0, mint #9dddbb, lilac #c9b8f3, a touch coral #e97c6b. Main object fills 85% of image and is fully visible with margins. Clean transparent background, no floor or backdrop. No readable words, no logos, no UI screenshot, no watermark. Warm, polished human illustration suitable for professional business software, not glossy 3D or stock corporate people.

`src/assets/illustrations/dashboard-preview.webp` is a captured rendering of this
project's Home screen with seeded demo data. It is a static product illustration,
not a second interactive dashboard. Update it deliberately when the workspace
layout changes. No Remote logos, product screenshots, ratings or trust claims were
copied into the product assets.
